Links & Prototype

Round 1: 3D Reconstruction & Roaming

01   Live web viewer

https://stardust-ns.vercel.app/

Open on a desktop or laptop browser. Wait for loading, then click Start walkthrough. Use WASD or arrow keys to walk, the mouse to look, Shift to move faster, Q/E to adjust eye height, R to reset and Esc to release the mouse.

Current prototype: full room2 reconstruction, all 1,474 registered frames at 1600×900, 60,000 training steps; 1,296,805 exported Gaussians and a 29.41 MiB SPZ model.

02   Code repository & reconstruction setup

https://github.com/positron-nb/starDust_NS

Branch: main. README.md contains viewer setup and reconstruction instructions; capture_sop.md describes data collection. The submitted model was trained locally in WSL2 on an RTX 5060. scripts/train_file_backed.py provides the memory-mapped image cache; docs/local_full_training.md documents the tested settings. The Colab notebook remains an alternative.

Sample data: test/sample_data/. Usage: test/README.md.
