1. Problem and proposed solution

Static 2D floor plans do not convey what an indoor space looks or feels like, and building 3D models manually does not scale. Namma Space is an end-to-end, software-only pipeline. A non-expert records one walkthrough video of a room on a smartphone following a fixed SOP. The pipeline then reconstructs a photorealistic 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) digital twin with no manual modelling and publishes it as a zero-install web page. Visitors can roam it in first person with WASD + mouse, like a video game.

Round 1 delivers capture, reconstruction and free roaming. The viewer is built on three.js, so Round 2 overlays (POIs, floor paths) attach to the same scene graph.

2. System architecture

3. Capture SOP and reconstruction methodology

3.1 Capture SOP (summary of capture_sop.md)

  • Space: one furnished room, 10–30 m², even lighting. Remove moving objects and private items. Cover mirrors.
  • Phone: main 1× lens only. Video at 4K30. AE/AF locked. No zoom and no "super-steady" mode, so the camera keeps one intrinsic model.
  • Motion: about 0.3 m/s, always translating while turning (parallax for SfM), chest height, tilted 10–15° down, 1–3 m from surfaces.
  • Route: entrance view, then an outer loop facing the centre with 3–5 s corner pauses, then an inner loop facing the walls, then furniture arcs at two heights, then back to the entrance (loop closure).
  • Failure prevention: a table maps blur, reflections, textureless walls, moving objects and exposure drift to their symptoms and fixes.

3.2 Processing

  1. Frame selection: decode at 6 fps and keep the frame with the highest variance of Laplacian in each group of 3. This gives 2 fps with even spacing and less motion blur.
  2. Quality gate: reject a frame if sharpness < max(40, 0.35 × median), brightness falls outside 35–225, clipped pixels exceed 25 %, fewer than 15 % of 32-px blocks are textured, or its 16×16 dHash is within 6 bits of the last kept frame.
  3. SfM: COLMAP via ns-process-data, with sequential matching for video and exhaustive matching for photo sets. Accept the run if registered ÷ used ≥ 0.8.
  4. 3DGS training: ns-train splatfacto, 30 k iterations, Gaussians initialised from SfM points.
  5. Export and compression: ns-export gaussian-splat → PLY, then splat-transform --filter-nan → SOG.
  6. Alignment: the viewer's ?calibrate panel levels the floor (z-up → y-up), sets metric scale from a known dimension, and estimates floor height and walk bounds from 2nd/98th-percentile splat centres.

6. Web viewer and interaction design

  • Start overlay with a key legend. The Start button is enabled only once the model has decoded. The progress bar shows downloaded MB and percentage.
  • Movement: pointer-lock mouse-look (pitch clamped to ±85°) and WASD/arrow keys at 1.4 m/s (Shift 2×). Exponential velocity smoothing gives fluid starts and stops. The camera is held at floor + eye height (1.6 m, Q/E ±0.6 m).
  • Navigation bound: the camera is clamped to the reconstructed footprint. This is explicitly not collision detection; splats have no surfaces.
  • Controls and robustness: R reset, Esc pause and resume, an exit button, fullscreen, and a HUD (FPS, splats, size, load time, position). There is a drag-to-look fallback when pointer lock is refused. Clear error cards cover a missing manifest or model, HTML 404 fallbacks, no WebGL2, and GPU context loss. Responsive down to 375 px.

8. Limitations, reproducibility and roadmap

Limitations

  • No physical collision. You can walk through furniture; only the footprint bound applies.
  • COLMAP is scale-free, so metric scale is set manually from a known dimension.
  • Textureless walls, mirrors and windows can produce floaters or holes. The SOP mitigates this, but splats do not fully solve it.
  • Large models require compression and possibly external hosting.

Reproducibility

Exact version pins (npm lockfile, nerfstudio 1.1.5), one script per platform plus a Colab notebook, metrics written automatically, and sample data with provenance in test/.

Roadmap

  • Round 2: a floor occupancy grid from splat centres and COLMAP points (height slice), A* paths drawn as a floor ribbon, collision against the same grid, POIs stored in the manifest with click-to-label (raycast on the splat mesh) and search-to-jump.
  • Quality: depth and normal priors (DN-Splatter) and line and plane cues (IndoorGS [3]) for flat indoor walls.
  • Scale: multi-room capture with doorway linking passes and vocab-tree matching.
  • Round 3 bonus: WebXR AR navigation, localising the phone against the SfM map.
